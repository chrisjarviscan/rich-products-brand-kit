## Logo

- The Rich's logo is the white serif "RICH'S" inside the red **pillow** shape, with a registered ® mark.
- Use the **Rich's Red positive version** on white or light backgrounds and the **reverse version** on dark backgrounds. If the ® is legible, you have the right version.
- One-color black or white versions are only for limited reproduction (faxes, newsprint). Never on product labels or advertising.
- Logos may be resized only. Color and design elements may not change.
- Check the ® requirement for your country with ip@rich.com.

### Clear space and minimum size
- Minimum clear space around the logo = the height of the "H" in the logo. More is fine, never less.
- Minimum size: **0.75 inches wide in print, 54 px wide on screen.**
- Exceptions need approval from Rich's brand team.

### Tagline (brand signature)
- **INFINITE POSSIBILITIES. ONE FAMILY.™** It is its own trademark.
- Two versions: two-line and single-line. "INFINITE POSSIBILITIES." in a lighter weight, "ONE FAMILY." in bold.
- Logo plus tagline lockups: **Stack Left**, **Horizontal**, **Stack Center** (Stack Center is the alternate, for multi-page documents, video and environments). Use only these arrangements and proportions. See `assets/rules/lockups-stack-left-horizontal-stack-center.png`.
- When locked up with the logo, the tagline does not need ™ or ®.
- Language versions: Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Thai, Chinese, Korean. The RPC version is used in Asian markets (Chinese and Korean). See `assets/logos/`.

### Placement and size
The logo is always anchored **top left**, on the margin. Logo width depends on the format's shortest edge (x):

| Format | Rule | Logo width |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (short edge more than half the long edge, e.g. letter, A4) | divide short edge into 5 | 20% of x |
| Narrow (short edge less than half the long edge, e.g. web banners) | divide short edge into 3 | 33% of x |
| Square (e.g. 1080 x 1080) | divide short edge into 4 | 25% of x |

### Digital use
- Top left on digital banners and layouts, following clear space rules.
- Over photos in web headers and footers, use the reverse logo on a dark-tinted image.
- Global Markets are encouraged to use a watermark logo on product imagery for social (not flags).

### Logo misuse (never)
Never use the logo without the pillow; never change the size of the wordmark inside the pillow; never skew, stretch or change proportions; never change its color; never add words or build new lockups (e.g. "BUSINESS UNIT"); never add shadows or effects. See `assets/rules/logo-misuse.png` and `assets/rules/application-misuse.png`.

### Legal
- Any departure from the brand guide needs approval from Global Branding & Legal.
- New brand assets or brand activity (e.g. licensing) need Brand Trust & Legal approval.
- Use the trademark as an adjective, consistently, with the proper ™ or ® notice. Confirm status with ip@rich.com for the product and country.

## Typography (from RW summary notes; confirm against portal)
- **Alright Sans Black**: primary brand headline font (agency use).
- **Verdana Bold**: system font for digital, web, email and PowerPoint, i.e. what RW should use.
- **Market Pro / Drina**: accent only.
- Web fallback stack: `Verdana, "Inter", Geneva, sans-serif`.

## Design elements and layout (from RW summary notes)
- **Pillow frame**: the rounded pillow shape is a protected brand element, also used to frame callouts.
- **Monoline icons**: thin-line icons, often inside the pillow frame.
- **45° angle**: diagonal cuts and bands are a signature layout move.
- **Graphic band**: 1/8-height red gradient band at top or bottom. **Content band**: 1/3-width vertical red band.
- Grid with margins, hang lines, columns and gutters. Generous white space. Colored (non-white) backgrounds preferred for hero areas.
- Personality: simple, bold, straightforward; approachable with technical expertise.
