# Rich Products brand kit

This repository holds Rich's (Rich Products) brand rules and logo files for Realized Worth work.
When making anything for Rich's, follow `BRAND.md` and `skills/rich-brand/SKILL.md`, use logos from
`assets/logos/` unchanged, and use colors and fonts from `tokens/`. This is Rich's brand, not the RW
or RW Institute brand. Do not mix them.
