# Rich's brand

Read `BRAND.md` in this repository before building. Short version:

1. Logo: use the files in `assets/logos/` unchanged. Top left, on the margin. Never without the pillow, never recolored, stretched, shadowed or locked up with new words. Minimum 54 px wide on screen, 0.75 in in print. Clear space = height of the "H".
2. Logo width: 20% of the short edge (standard formats), 33% (narrow, e.g. banners), 25% (square).
3. Tagline: INFINITE POSSIBILITIES. ONE FAMILY.™ Only in the Stack Left, Horizontal or Stack Center lockups.
4. Colors: Rich's Red `#BA0C2F` leads; Dark Red `#980C31`, Light Red `#D31145` (all three confirmed). Gray `#58595B`, Black `#231F20`, Warm White `#F9F7F4` and the pastel chart colors are unconfirmed, so flag them as such in anything final. Tokens in `tokens/`.
5. Type: Verdana (bold for headings) for documents, email and slides, as Rich's own documents do. Rich's brand font is Alright Sans (licensed); use it only if Rich's supplies the files.
6. Look: simple and bold, generous white space, 45° angled cuts, red gradient bands, pillow-shaped callouts.
7. Exceptions or trademark questions go to Rich's Global Branding & Legal (ip@rich.com). Never invent new brand assets.
